Research

Research

I work with the Fuller and Kremer groups at UCSD. Right now that means looking at whether primordial black holes could get captured in our solar system (I use REBOUND for this), and separately, figuring out what gravitational wave signals come out of globular clusters that are packed with black holes, using a code called PeTar. Last summer I was a SPURS Scholar, which let me spend the whole summer modeling collisions between globular clusters.

Before that, I spent a summer working with USC and UC Irvine on galaxy formation, using the FIRE simulations to look at how Milky Way sized galaxies grow over time. I also did a data science fellowship through UT Arlington, researching nuclear fuel recycling.
